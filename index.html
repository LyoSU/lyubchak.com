About

Started at twelve, editing uCoz sites with too much time on my hands. Then came PHP, then VK bots, then the Telegram ecosystem I still live inside today.

Millions of people have used something I wrote, mostly through fStik and QuotLy. Once won first place at the official Telegram Mini App Contest, a cool milestone, but not the main focus anymore.

The main focus now is AI that actually works inside a real company. I do that at KNESS, a large manufacturing group, as AI lead: I work with teams across the company to find what's worth automating, build it, wire it into live systems, and see it through to stable production.

On the side I run my own bots and write at @LyBlog.
