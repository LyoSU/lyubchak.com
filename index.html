About

Started at twelve, editing uCoz sites with too much time on my hands. Then came PHP, then VK bots, then the Telegram ecosystem I still live inside today.

Most of those millions came through fStik and QuotLy. I also won first place at the official Telegram Mini App Contest. Useful proof, though production has taught me more than contests ever could.

I like work with consequences: systems that hold real data, save real hours and still have to behave on a bad Monday morning. That is the thread connecting enterprise AI, open-source experiments and Telegram products at scale.

I write about the work and what I learn at @LyBlog.

How I build
