About

Started at twelve, editing uCoz sites with too much time on my hands. Then came PHP, then VK bots, then the Telegram ecosystem I still live inside today.

Most of my public products grew out of Telegram. fStik and QuotLy together reached millions of users, and in 2023 I took first place in Telegram's Mini App Contest.

These days most of my work is around AI, infrastructure and automation, both at KNESS and in my own projects.

I still like building things myself and running them in production. I write about it at @LyBlog.

How I build

TypeScript / Node.js for most backend work, React / Next.js on the frontend, Python where it makes sense. Telegram through Telegraf, grammY, TDLib, MTProto and Mini Apps. Infrastructure is mostly Linux and Docker on my own boxes, with Postgres, MongoDB, Redis and Sentry
